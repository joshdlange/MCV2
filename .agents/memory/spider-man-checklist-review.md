name: Spider-Man owner checklist and review policy
description: Owner-approved scope and treatment of questionable owned entries for the 1995 Ultra and 2002 Topps repairs.
---
The owner supplied an 80-card 2002 Topps Spider-Man base checklist and specified that 1995 Fleer Ultra Spider-Man Base and Gold Signature must each contain ONLY the supplied 150 cards.

Questionable legacy entries should be separated into clearly labeled review subsets, retaining ownership and images rather than renaming them into unrelated cards or deleting them.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose “Separate for review” after learning that collectors owned entries matching a different year's checklist and a sealed-box entry.

**How to apply:** Keep review entries outside these official checklists. Do not guess a different year's physical parallel from a matching number/name; establish the actual card identity before any later relocation or merge.
