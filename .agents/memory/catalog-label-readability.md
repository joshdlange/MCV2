---
name: Catalog label readability
description: User preference for restrained card and subset tile typography changes.
---
Show subset names without cutting them off, using smaller readable text where needed. Keep cards aligned and standardized; card-name truncation is acceptable, but identification should remain easy. Avoid a broad redesign.

**Why:** The user found truncated names hard to identify while adding images and explicitly asked for modest changes, not a redesign.

**How to apply:** Preserve the compact catalog layout, allow subset labels to wrap, and keep identifying card numbers visible.

Use short collector-facing subset labels, with “Base Cards” for recognized base subsets. Show the master name once as context and separate Master Set and Subset in card details. Keep canonical stored names and ownership relationships unchanged.

**Why:** The owner approved display-only cleanup after seeing the Nahuel Grego master name repeated twice in Browse; full catalog naming should not crowd subset labels.

**How to apply:** Preserve distinguishing insert and parallel names; never classify an ambiguous long name as Base just to shorten it. Admin editing names remain canonical, but Browse tiles use short labels even when admin controls are enabled.

Card details should put compact set/subset information above the image and keep collection/wishlist actions accessible without scrolling.

**Why:** The owner said collectors may not scroll through card details, and the large metadata block hid the add buttons.

**How to apply:** Prioritize visible add actions over a large metadata panel; preserve the approved Browse heading with master context above the short subset name.

For owned cards, keep an explicit Remove action, visible minus/count/plus controls, and a compact wishlist heart in one fixed footer row. Collection actions should work the same from Browse, Search, and My Collection.

**Why:** The owner reported collectors could not discover removal and approved replacing the ambiguous owned-status button without expanding the details panel.

**How to apply:** Do not hide removal behind an ownership label or quantity adjustment behind a badge. Confirm removal of the last copy or all copies; keep ordinary quantity changes direct.
