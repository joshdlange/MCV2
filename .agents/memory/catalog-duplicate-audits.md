---
name: Catalog duplicate audits
description: How to distinguish true duplicate cards and subsets from legitimate repeated numbering.
---

Do not treat repeated card numbers inside a subset as automatically duplicate. Sketches, relics, autographs, and parallel checklists commonly reuse numbers. Exact normalized number + name + variation twins and identical subset names under the same parent are high-confidence candidates; differing identities require classification or manual review.

**Why:** A raw production scan produced thousands of repeated-number groups dominated by legitimate inserts, while real user-reported duplicates included both exact twins and a misplaced parallel with a different name. A number-only cleanup would destroy valid cards, while an exact-only audit misses misplaced rows.

**How to apply:** Resolve sets by slug, validate expected terminal checklists, merge through the reference-preserving batch path, and soft-archive rather than delete. Repoint collections, wishlists, binders, listings, XP, scans, feeds, and images atomically. Re-run terminal assertions after every cleanup.
