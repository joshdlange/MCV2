Suggested definitions:

- Onboarding Complete: signup/onboarding completed
- Empty Vault: onboarding complete but zero cards
- Collector Started: added at least one card
- Returning Collector: logged in 3+ times or active on 3+ days
- Engaged Collector: meaningful activity such as 10+ cards, PC Binder, wishlist, image upload, or share
- Power Collector: high collection/activity threshold such as 100+ cards or repeated sessions
- Super Hero: active paid subscriber
- Cancelled: was Super Hero and now cancelled
- Dormant: no activity in 30+ days

These definitions can be adjusted based on existing data structure, but document the exact rules used.
