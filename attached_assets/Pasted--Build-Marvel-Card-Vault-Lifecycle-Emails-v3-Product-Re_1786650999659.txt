Email 1: Payment Failed / Subscription Interrupted

Type:
Transactional/revenue protection.

Audience:

* user has failed payment, past_due, unpaid, or subscription payment issue if data exists from Stripe/RevenueCat
* active or recently active subscriber
* has not already received this exact payment-failed notice for the same billing issue if trackable

Purpose:
Help user fix billing and keep Super Hero access.

Subject:
Action needed: keep your Super Hero access

Preview text:
There was an issue with your payment. Update billing to keep your vault upgraded.

Body guidance:
Keep this short, direct, and helpful. Tell the user there was an issue with their payment and that updating billing helps avoid losing Super Hero access.

CTA:
Update Billing

Rules:

* This may be transactional and should not be blocked by the 14-day marketing cap.
* Do not send if billing issue data is not available or reliable.
* If billing update URL cannot be generated safely, report the blocker.
* Do not change Stripe or RevenueCat billing logic.
* Do not create unsafe billing links.
* Do not expose Stripe or RevenueCat secrets.

Email 2: Subscription Cancelled / Super Hero Expired

Type:
Lifecycle/revenue.

Audience:

* user was Super Hero and now cancelled or expired
* not currently active Super Hero
* has not already received this cancellation/expired email for the current cancellation event

Purpose:
Win back or remind them what they lose.

Subject:
Your Super Hero access has ended

Preview text:
You can come back anytime and keep building without limits.

Body guidance:
Remind them they can restart anytime. Mention unlimited cards, PC Binders, Market Trends, Scan to Add, and collector tools, but do not sound desperate.

CTA:
Restart Super Hero

Rules:

* Respect marketing preferences unless this is purely account status.
* Do not use BABYCOMEBACK here unless explicitly approved later.
* Do not claim they lost data.
* Do not change subscription behavior.

Email 3: Near 500-Card Side Kick Limit

Type:
High-intent upgrade lifecycle.

Audience:

* Side Kick user
* card count at or above 400, or 80% of the 500-card limit
* not Super Hero
* has not already received this near-limit email
* has not received another lifecycle/marketing email within 14 days
* opted into marketing/lifecycle emails if preferences exist

Purpose:
Upgrade before they hit the wall.

Subject:
You’re building fast

Preview text:
You’re getting close to the Side Kick card limit. Super Hero gives you unlimited space.

Body guidance:
Mention they are nearing the Side Kick 500-card limit. Do not scare them. Position Super Hero as unlimited cards plus PC Binders, Market Trends, Scan to Add, and more room to keep building.

CTA:
Upgrade to Super Hero

Email 4: PC Binder Prompt for Super Hero Users

Type:
Product engagement lifecycle.

Audience:

* has at least 10 cards
* has zero PC Binders
* has not already received this PC Binder prompt

* opted into marketing/lifecycle emails if preferences exist

Drive usage of a paid feature.

Build a binder around your favorites

Preview text:
Create a PC Binder for a character, artist, set, or chase list.

Body guidance:

CTA:
Create a PC Binder

Email 5: PC Binder Upgrade Nudge

Type:
Upgrade lifecycle.

Audience:

* clicked PC Binders, viewed PC Binder gate, or attempted to create/view gated PC Binder

Convert users who show intent.

Body guidance:

CTA:
Unlock PC Binders
