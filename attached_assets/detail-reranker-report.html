The first Hough-only visual-QA pass was archived; the existing conservative detector was retained ahead of Hough before inference. No retrieval-label tuning or score-based crop selection occurred. The reranker weights stayed fixed.

This is an offline human QA gate, not a proven automatic confidence system. Geometry scores alone would have accepted bad crops. The experiment therefore cannot claim reliable automatic isolation. Stage2: fixed, label-free detail evidence

Only each query's DINO top10 is evaluated. A coarse-to-fine 16×24 local spatial descriptor searches grayscale normalized cross-correlation across scale, translation and reference rotations0/90/180/270. Reliable alignment requires NCC≥0.48 and best-versus-coarse-runner-up gap≥0.012. Reference/query RGB regions are compared only after that gate. This is not a full invariant local-feature matcher; severe perspective, glare and low resolution defeat it.

Final score = 0.65×DINO cosine +0.35×detail. Detail combines 35% border color, 25% spatial color regions, 25% edge/layout and 15% luminance structure. If alignment fails, detail contribution equals the DINO score, preserving that candidate's original score instead of injecting unaligned color. Scores are not probabilities. No OCR, decoded card number, character identity, year/set/number metadata, paid model or external AI call is used.

Candidate-specific scores can still be affected by mismatched lighting, background and spurious alignment; an NCC gate is not proof of semantic correspondence. Numeric thresholds/weights were frozen before label scoring and used identically on every case, with no per-card exceptions. Reference features are cached; originals/catalog references are never overwritten.
