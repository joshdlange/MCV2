If:
- main set evidence conflicts

- logos/marks

- front design differences

Do not rely mostly on character/artwork similarity.

5. CONFIDENCE SHOULD MEAN SOMETHING

Audit the current confidence calculation.

High confidence should require strong agreement among multiple reliable signals.

Suggested philosophy:

HIGH:
- card number agrees
- year/set strongly agree
- subset/parallel agrees or visual comparison strongly supports it
- catalog image is a strong match
- no major conflicting evidence

MEDIUM:
- likely family identified
- one or more important fields uncertain
- multiple plausible parallel/subset candidates remain

LOW:
- weak OCR
- weak visual match
- conflicting metadata
- missing catalog imagery
- multiple plausible candidates

If the system is unsure, SAY IT IS UNSURE.

A low-confidence shortlist is better than confidently adding the wrong card.
