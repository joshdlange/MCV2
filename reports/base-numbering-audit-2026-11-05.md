# Base checklist numbering audit

Production read-only snapshot, November 5, 2026. No catalog records changed by this audit.

Scope: 379 base-labeled/identified candidate subsets; 185 populated and 194 empty. Empty placeholders are not proof a checklist is missing; some represent promo-only products or duplicate catalog shells.

43 populated candidates have duplicate numeric positions, interior gaps, or mixed numeric/non-numeric entries. These are review flags, NOT a verified deletion list. Inserts/variations can be legitimate. Numbering alone cannot establish an expected final card number or detect incorrect names in an otherwise complete sequence. Continued series need not start at 1; prefixed checklists require separate validation.
