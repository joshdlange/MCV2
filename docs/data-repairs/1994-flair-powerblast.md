# 1994 Flair Marvel Annual — PowerBlast root cause

## Development verification (2026-09-29)

The development catalog was already archived by an earlier merge. Running the strengthened repair twice preserved 46 owned copies and 44 XP events; wishlist, binder, and feed references were zero before and after. Rollback-only regression fixtures also exercise the full unarchived 20-to-18 merge, overlapping ownership, metadata conflicts, reference transfers, and malformed-checklist rejection. After app restart, the public subset list returned only the canonical 18-card PowerBlast subset, and its picker returned all 18 inserts. Production application remains through the existing published startup repair, not a direct production write.

The set/subset taxonomy CSV labels this insert **`Flair Marvel Universe PowerBlast`** (under `1994 Flair Marvel Annual`, year 1994), without a hyphen before PowerBlast. The card checklist CSV instead labels the same 18 cards **`Flair Marvel Universe - PowerBlast`**; its full-set-name field is `1994 Flair Marvel Annual - Flair Marvel Universe - PowerBlast`. These are different source spellings of one insert, not two checklists. The checklist has cards numbered 1–18; in particular, Cyclops is #2, Storm is #6, Punisher is #10, and Spider-Man is #15. See `attached_assets/Marvel_Sets_&_Subsets_REDO_-_Sets_To_Sub_Sets_(2)_1769868071902.csv` (row 34) and `attached_assets/Marvel_Cards_Master_collection_1_-_Sets_scratch_(3)_1770047270123.csv` (card rows 410–427).

The structural CSV importer (`scripts/phase1-import-csv-structure.ts`) constructs subset names as `main set - subset` and slugs from `year-main set-subset`, lowercasing and replacing punctuation/whitespace with hyphens. Its taxonomy row therefore produces **`1994 Flair Marvel Annual - Flair Marvel Universe PowerBlast`**, slug **`1994-1994-flair-marvel-annual-flair-marvel-universe-powerblast`**: the repeated `1994` comes from the explicit year and the year already in the main-set name. The other, legacy subset has slug **`1994-flair-marvel-annual-flair-marvel-universe-powerblast`**. The hyphen difference in the displayed source names normalizes away in these slugs, but the year-prefix difference does not, so slug-based matching can treat them as separate subsets. Both exact slugs are recorded as source and target respectively in `server/seeds/mergeDuplicateLegacySets.ts` and `server/tests/legacy-set-merge-regression.test.ts`; do not infer that the target's display name contains the checklist's extra hyphen from its slug alone.

The punctuation-name/structural duplicate contains **20 erroneous rows**, not a second 20-card insert. Relative to the **correct 18-card checklist**, its extra #2 Punisher conflicts with canonical #2 Cyclops and actually corresponds to canonical #10 Punisher; its extra #6 Spider-Man conflicts with canonical #6 Storm and corresponds to canonical #15 Spider-Man. `exports/duplicate_card_numbers_by_subset.csv` flags the #2 and #6 name collisions. This is a catalog/import identity error, not an additional PowerBlast parallel.

The dedicated `repair1994FlairPowerBlast` in `server/seeds/mergeDuplicateLegacySets.ts` now replaces PowerBlast's former generic `nameOnlyFallback`. It requires exactly 18 active target identities (#1–18 with expected names) and exactly 20 source rows: the same 18 number/name pairs plus **only** the two explicitly mapped bad-number exceptions above. Unexpected identities fail the transaction rather than silently matching by name. It transfers references (including references added to already-archived source cards), copies source images only into empty target slots, preserves metadata for absorbed collection rows, records per-card audit entries, and archives/deactivates the redundant source. It marks the surviving 18-card subset canonical/insert and checks terminal card counts, metadata, and zero remaining source references; reruns validate the archived source too.

## Normal browse and picker visibility

`server/storage.ts` excludes inactive/archived subsets from `/api/card-sets`. General `/api/cards` (`server/optimized-storage.ts`) requires unarchived cards in active, unarchived subsets even without search. Both list and count in `/api/sets/:setId/cards`, and both search and non-search paths in `/api/cards/picker/cards` (`server/routes.ts`), enforce those same card/subset conditions; picker year/set/subset listings already exclude inactive/archived subsets. Thus a retired PowerBlast source does not appear in normal browsing or pickers. Separate UI followups: selected-set search and insert/base controls are not applied by `client/src/components/cards/card-grid.tsx`; the PC binder Add Cards dialog in `client/src/pages/pc-binder-detail.tsx` uses text search rather than a set/subset picker.
